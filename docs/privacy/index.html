Privacy & cookies

How ATD handles project enquiries and contact information.

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who is responsible

AT DERMYSHI LTD, trading as ATD North London Painters, is the controller. Company number 16124001; registered office 94 Lausanne Road, London, England, N8 0HP. Contact tdermyshi88@gmail.com or 07557 954457 about your information or rights.

Project enquiry information

The live project form collects your name, email, phone, property address and postcode, selected service, project specifications and any optional information you choose to provide. We use this to respond, assess the work, arrange the next step and prepare a quotation. The form requires contact details but does not require email confirmation. Supplied email addresses, phone numbers, identity and property ownership are not verified.

Why we use it

Processing needed to assess and quote for work you request is undertaken to take steps towards a contract at your request. Managing correspondence, preventing spam and protecting the enquiry service also serve our legitimate business interests. An enquiry does not sign you up for unrelated marketing.

Submitting your enquiry

After submission, your enquiry is saved for ATD to review and an owner notification is attempted. You do not need to confirm your email. Keep the private enquiry-status link shown after submission; the service stores a hash of its access token. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed appointment, contract or agreed price. Older email-confirmation links remain supported until they expire.

Services handling your information

The enquiry system uses Supabase for processing and storing project enquiries, and Resend for transactional email delivery. Notifications and correspondence may be received through our business email provider. The standalone form and dashboard may be hosted through GitHub Pages. Provider arrangements, including any international transfers and safeguards, must be considered for the live service. Contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your enquiry.

Spam and security checks

The live service checks required fields, uses a hidden spam field and limits repeated requests. Short-lived rate counters use salted hashes of email and, where available, network address information; raw network addresses are not retained in those counters. Rate counters expire within their request window and are cleared during subsequent rate-limit processing.

Photos and contact channels

After sending your enquiry, you can attach up to four project photos using your private enquiry link. Photos are resized on your device and stored privately with the enquiry in Supabase. Only the ATD owner can request short-lived viewing links. You can also send photos separately on WhatsApp, using your name or enquiry reference so they can be matched to the work. WhatsApp and other contact channels have their own privacy practices. Please avoid sending identity documents, bank details or sensitive personal information with an initial enquiry.

Retention

We retain information for the enquiry and, where work proceeds, for the resulting contract and applicable legal, accounting and dispute-handling purposes. Retention depends on whether an enquiry remains active, leads to work, or needs to be retained for those obligations. You can ask us what is held, the applicable period and whether it can be deleted. Older confirmation links stop working when they expire; their enquiry records are not automatically deleted solely because a link expires.

Cookies and your device

The public website and guided form do not use advertising or analytics cookies, embedded social feeds, or automatic marketing consent. We count page views and form starts by broad source (such as Google, Instagram or ChatGPT), and saved enquiries, to understand the website. Historical email-confirmation counts may include earlier technical tests. These are activity counts, not unique visitors. Browsing counts respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not send page query values, form answers or client identifiers to the counting endpoint. Temporary hashed network counters limit misuse of the endpoint. Form answers stay in memory while you complete the page and are not saved to browser storage by the public form. The private owner dashboard uses sign-in/session storage separately. Social icons are external links. When you follow them, the destination has its own terms and privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, or object to processing. If processing relies on consent you may withdraw it. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
