BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions,
answered.

Can you paint just one door or one room?+

Yes. For a door, tell us whether you need one or both sides painted and whether the frame is included. For a room, tell us which walls, ceilings or woodwork need attention. You can ask for advice about colours and finishes.

Who supplies the paint?+

We can discuss the products you have chosen or the finish you want to achieve. Supply arrangements and material costs are agreed in the quotation.

Can I stay at home while you work?+

Often, but this depends on access, drying times and the work involved. We agree practical arrangements with you before starting.
