Website terms

A straightforward guide to using this website.

Enquiries and quotations

Website content describes the services we can discuss. Sending an enquiry, creating a WhatsApp draft or using the Business Hub form does not create a confirmed booking or contract. Work, materials, exclusions, price, payment arrangements and timings must be agreed in writing.

Your project

Photos show individual projects and finishes. They do not mean every pictured fitting or item is included in a service. Suitability, access, permissions and any specialist requirements are assessed for your property.

Your statutory rights are unaffected by these website terms or the labour offer.
