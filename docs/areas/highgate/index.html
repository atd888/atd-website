DECORATING IN HIGHGATE

Inside and out.
Finished properly.

Highgate has many older houses with sash windows, timber doors and painted render. Exterior work on these surfaces needs the right preparation and coatings, and safe access planned in advance. We cover all of this in your written quotation.

Parts of Highgate are in conservation areas. If you are planning to change the colour of a front door or the front of the house, check whether any restrictions apply before work is booked.
