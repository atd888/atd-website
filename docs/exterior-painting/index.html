BE SPECIFIC ABOUT THE SURFACES

What needs
a fresh finish?

A front door and a whole house front
need different briefs.

01

Front doors & entrances

Tell us whether the door, frame and surrounding painted surfaces are included, and whether you want both sides painted. A close-up of the existing finish helps us discuss preparation and colour choices.

02

Timber windows & frames

Show the frames, sills and their current condition. Include the number of windows, their height and how they can be reached. Repairs and any additional preparation need to be assessed and expressly included in the scope.

03

House fronts & exterior walls

Send a wide photograph of the elevation and closer views of damaged or peeling areas. Identify the surfaces you want painted and any areas to leave untouched. Condition, coatings and access are considered before quoting.

04

Railings & exterior metalwork

Share photographs and approximate lengths or quantities. Existing coatings, surface condition and the finish you want determine the preparation and paint system to discuss.

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Exterior painting
questions.

Can I ask for just a front door?+

Yes. Say whether the frame, surrounding painted surfaces and one or both sides of the door are included. Send photographs and your postcode for the next step.

Do you paint exterior timber windows?+

Yes. Share the number of windows, height, access and photographs of the frames and sills. Condition and any repairs must be assessed before the preparation and scope are agreed.

Are scaffolding and repairs included?+

Only where identified and agreed in your written quotation. Tell us about height, access and damaged areas so we can discuss the arrangements and responsibilities before work starts.

How much will exterior painting cost?+

The price depends on the surfaces, condition, preparation, materials and access. ATD quotes for the agreed work at your property. Photos help us begin; a visit may be needed before a final written quotation.

Can you work around the weather?+

Weather and the requirements of the chosen coatings are considered when planning exterior work. Timings are discussed for your project rather than promised from a photograph alone.
