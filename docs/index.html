A LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can I send you photos to get started?+

Absolutely. Send photos on WhatsApp, along with your postcode and a short description of the work. A visit may be needed before a final written quotation.

Do you offer free quotations?+

Yes. Get in touch with the project details and we will discuss the next step.

What if I do not know all the details?+

Share what you know and choose “Not sure” where you need advice. Measurements and chosen colours can be discussed before your quotation is agreed.

How does the 10% offer work?+

Ask for the website offer when you enquire. It applies to the labour element of the agreed quotation only; materials are excluded. See the offer page for the terms.

Can I use your online enquiry form?+

Yes. Select Request a quote to open the guided project form. Your enquiry is ready for review after you send it; no email confirmation is needed. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking or commit you to work.
