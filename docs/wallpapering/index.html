BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions,
answered.

Can you hang wallpaper I already own?+

Yes. Send the product details and quantity you have. We will discuss the wall condition and whether there is enough paper, including pattern matching and waste.

Do you prepare the walls first?+

We assess the surface and include the agreed preparation in your quotation. Some walls may need additional work before wallpaper can be hung.

Can you help with just one feature wall?+

Yes. A single feature wall is welcome; send its dimensions and your chosen covering.
