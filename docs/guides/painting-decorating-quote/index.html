THE WORK BEHIND THE PRICE

What affects a painting
and decorating quote?

1. The surfaces included

“Paint the room” can mean walls only, or walls, ceiling, skirting, doors and frames. List each surface. For a door, say whether the frame and both sides are included. Approximate room dimensions and ceiling height help describe the scale.

2. The existing condition

Sound painted walls need a different scope from surfaces with peeling paint, cracks or old wallpaper. Send close-ups as well as a whole-room photograph. Wallpaper stripping or more extensive repairs should be identified separately; the surface underneath may need assessing before the preparation can be confirmed.

3. Paint, colour and finish

Tell us whether you want to keep the existing colour or make a change, and whether you have selected a product. Include the finish for walls and woodwork, or say you need advice. The quotation should identify the agreed paint system, coverage and who supplies the materials.

4. Access and protection

Furniture, an occupied room, stairs, parking and access to upper floors can affect how the work is organised. Explain what can be moved and whether the property will remain in use. Agree protection and moving responsibilities before work starts.

5. Exterior condition and access

For exterior decorating, show the whole elevation and close-ups of doors, windows and damaged areas. Height, safe access, surface condition and weather affect the plan. Ask whether access equipment and any separately agreed repairs are included.

6. The sequence of work

Tell us your preferred timeframe and whether other trades are involved. Preparation, coats and drying need to be considered together. A requested date is a starting point for discussion; availability and the programme are agreed separately.

COMPARE THE SAME WORK

Look beyond
the total price.

Give each decorator the same brief. A lower total may cover fewer surfaces, less preparation or different materials. Before accepting a quotation, check:

  • Scope: the rooms and surfaces included, and anything excluded.
  • Preparation: the agreed repairs, stripping, sanding and priming, where required.
  • Materials: products, finishes, supply responsibilities and any allowances.
  • Practical arrangements: access equipment, protection, furniture, working access and clearing up.
  • Price and changes: what the total includes, payment arrangements, any applicable tax and how additional work will be agreed and priced.
  • Timing: the proposed start, sequence and any conditions that affect the programme.

A photograph can start the conversation. A visit may be needed before a final written quotation, especially where condition or access is unclear. Agree changes to the scope before the additional work proceeds.

MAKE YOUR FIRST ENQUIRY USEFUL

What to send
with your quote request.

  1. Your postcode and property type.
  2. The rooms or exterior areas, with approximate dimensions where known.
  3. The surfaces to include: walls, ceilings, doors, frames, skirting or exterior woodwork.
  4. A whole-space photo, close-ups of problem areas and a note about old wallpaper or peeling paint.
  5. Your colour and finish preferences, or “not sure — please advise”.
  6. Access, whether the space is occupied and your preferred timeframe.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Decorating quote
questions, answered.

Can you give a decorating price from photographs?+

Photographs help us understand the size and condition of the work. We may need measurements, further details or a visit before a final written quotation. The agreed scope and price are specific to your property.

Is paint included in the quote?+

Supply arrangements are agreed for each project. Check that the quotation identifies the materials, finish and who supplies them. Tell us if you have already bought paint or wallpaper.

Can I ask for one room or just exterior woodwork?+

Yes. You can enquire about one room, doors or exterior woodwork. Specify the surfaces and preparation needed so the quotation covers the work you actually want.

Does the website offer apply to materials?+

No. The website offer is 10% off the labour element identified in your agreed quotation. Ask for it when you enquire, before accepting. Materials are excluded; see the offer terms.
