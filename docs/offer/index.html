WINTER OFFER · 1 DECEMBER 2026 – 1 MARCH 2027

10% off labour.
Fresh rooms for the new year.

Get 10% off the labour on interior painting, decorating and wallpapering carried out between 1 December 2026 and 1 March 2027. Mention the winter offer when you enquire. Materials are excluded.

Request my winter quotation↗

How the offer works

  • The offer covers interior painting, decorating and wallpapering work carried out between 1 December 2026 and 1 March 2027. The dates are agreed in your written quotation.
  • The discount is 10% of the labour charges identified in your agreed written quotation.
  • Materials and other non-labour costs are excluded from the discount.
  • Ask for the website offer when enquiring, before accepting the quotation.
  • The written quotation must show the labour amount and discount so you can see the saving before you agree.
  • The offer does not confirm availability, a booking or a fixed project price. The scope and price are assessed for your property.
  • Other terms, payment arrangements and any applicable tax are set out in the quotation. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
